2  PERSON-CENTERED COMMUNICATION IN THE AI ERA

15 Weeks × 2 Hours per Week

2.1 1. Course Positioning

This course treats communication not merely as transmission of information, but as a person-centered process for creating, maintaining, repairing, and growing relationships.

The course develops the earlier communication architecture—Creative Individual, Synergy Professional, and Conscious Citizen—into a broader five-domain framework while retaining experiential learning, roleplay, AI-mediated communication, and performance-based assessment.

The five domains are:

  1. Intra-Self
  2. Family and Friends
  3. Working Colleagues
  4. Neighbors and Communities
  5. The World

The central model is:

PERSON → PERSONALITY → NARRATIVE CHARACTER → OBJECTIVE → REPERTOIRE → LANGUAGE → ACTION → AGREEMENT → RELATIONSHIP

Communication competence therefore means:

The ability to recognize persons and their narrative characters, understand their objectives and current states, select appropriate repertoires and languages, and communicate toward ethical agreements and constructive action.

3 2. Central Communication Model

3.1 2.1 The Five Communication Domains

3.1.1 Domain 1 — Intra-Self

ME ↔︎ ME

Purpose: - self-understanding - reflection - self-regulation - decision - commitment - personal development

3.1.2 Domain 2 — Family and Friends

ME ↔︎ PEOPLE CLOSE TO ME

Purpose: - belonging - trust - affection - mutual understanding - reconciliation - relationship growth

3.1.3 Domain 3 — Working Colleagues

PROFESSIONAL ↔︎ PROFESSIONAL

Purpose: - cooperation - coordination - value creation - negotiation - problem solving - agreement

3.1.4 Domain 4 — Neighbors and Communities

CITIZEN ↔︎ COMMUNITY

Purpose: - coexistence - participation - social cooperation - consensus - conflict resolution - collective action

3.1.5 Domain 5 — The World

PERSON ↔︎ PUBLIC

Purpose: - informing - teaching - influencing - inspiring - contributing - creating social impact

4 3. The Baseball Model of Communication — TAIDA

Purposeful communication is treated as a state-transition process.

4.1 TARGET

Identify the person, character, situation, problem, or opportunity.

↓

4.2 BASE 1 — ATTENTION

“I notice that you have a problem or opportunity.”

The receiver recognizes:

“Yes, this concerns me.”

↓

4.3 BASE 2 — INTEREST

“I have something that may help.”

The receiver responds:

“Tell me more.”

↓

4.4 BASE 3 — DESIRE

“This is how it works and why it matters to you.”

The receiver thinks:

“I would like this outcome.”

↓

4.5 HOME BASE — ACTION

“It is feasible. Shall we proceed?”

The communication closes with:

Agreement → Commitment → Action

Thus:

T → A → I → D → A

Communication pitches are interventions designed to move the relationship from one state to the next.

5 4. AI-Mediated Communication

AI is treated as a communication copilot, not as the owner of the relationship.

The basic architecture is:

PERSON + AI COPILOT ⇄ PERSON + AI COPILOT

AI may assist with:

  • understanding another person’s perspective
  • recognizing narrative character
  • preparing communication
  • adapting language
  • translating jargon
  • analyzing tone
  • generating alternatives
  • roleplaying difficult conversations
  • tracking TAIDA state
  • identifying unresolved issues
  • summarizing agreements
  • assisting negotiation

In selected situations:

Agent A ⇄ Agent B

may negotiate within explicitly delegated authority.

Human persons remain responsible for consequential decisions.

6 5. Course Learning Outcomes

By the end of the semester, students should be able to:

6.0.1 CLO 1 — Recognize Persons

Identify the person, personality tendencies, narrative character, relationship, and communication context.

6.0.2 CLO 2 — Define Communication Objectives

Determine what relationship or behavioral state should change through communication.

6.0.3 CLO 3 — Select Repertoire

Choose appropriate communication instruments: speaking, listening, questioning, writing, storytelling, visualization, negotiation, silence, media, or AI assistance.

6.0.4 CLO 4 — Adapt Language

Translate the same underlying meaning into language appropriate to different persons and characters.

6.0.5 CLO 5 — Apply TAIDA

Design communication pitches that move a person from Target toward Attention, Interest, Desire, and Action.

6.0.6 CLO 6 — Reach Agreements

Use communication and negotiation to establish acceptable agreements enabling coordinated action.

6.0.7 CLO 7 — Grow Relationships

Evaluate communication not merely by message delivery but by effects on trust and relationships.

6.0.8 CLO 8 — Use AI Ethically

Use AI-mediated communication for understanding and empowerment rather than manipulation.

7 6. Weekly Structure

Each 2-hour session generally consists of:

20 min — Concept
20 min — Case/story
30 min — Demonstration
40 min — Student simulation/practice
10 min — AI-assisted reflection

The emphasis is:

Learn → Perform → Observe → Reflect → Improve

24 8. Communication Performance Rubric

Each major exercise can use five dimensions.

24.1 A. Person & Character Recognition — 20%

Does the student understand who is actually participating?

24.2 B. Objective & State Recognition — 20%

Does the student know what needs to change and where the other person is in TAIDA?

24.3 C. Repertoire & Language — 20%

Are communication method and language appropriate?

24.4 D. Agreement & Action — 20%

Does communication lead toward a clear and acceptable outcome?

24.5 E. Relationship & Ethics — 20%

Does communication preserve autonomy, dignity, trust, and long-term relationship value?

25 9. Semester Developmental Arc

The semester deliberately moves outward:

25.0.1 Weeks 1–2

ME

Understand and communicate with myself.

25.0.2 Weeks 3–4

ME ↔︎ YOU

Understand intimate persons and adapt language.

25.0.3 Weeks 5–11

WE WORK

Discover needs, create value, pitch, negotiate, and agree.

25.0.4 Week 12

WE LIVE TOGETHER

Communicate across differences and create collective action.

25.0.5 Weeks 13–14

WE ADDRESS THE WORLD

Communicate publicly and augment communication with AI.

25.0.6 Week 15

INTEGRATION

Become a complete person-centered communicator.

Therefore:

SELF → RELATIONSHIP → PROFESSIONAL VALUE → COMMUNITY → WORLD

26 10. The Course’s Unifying Formula

The entire course can be summarized as:

[ ]

operating through:

[ ]

to produce:

[ ]

with:

[ ]

rather than:

[ AI = Replacement for the Person ]

The central educational proposition is therefore:

A competent communicator recognizes persons rather than merely categories, understands the narrative characters they are playing, selects language and repertoire appropriate to the relationship, moves communication constructively through TAIDA, and reaches ethical agreements that enable action and grow relationships.

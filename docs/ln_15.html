18  MINGGU 15 — CAPSTONE: THE COMPLETE PERSON-CENTERED COMMUNICATOR

TipMengintegrasikan Person, Character, Relationship, TAIDA, Negotiation, Community, Public Communication, dan AI

Mata Kuliah: Person-Centered Communication in the AI Era Tingkat: Mahasiswa Tahun 2 Durasi: 2 jam Jenis Pertemuan: Final Integration & Capstone Performance Tema Besar: Can I recognize persons, communicate across contexts, create shared understanding, reach responsible agreements, and use AI without losing human agency?

18.1 1. Minggu 15 Bukan Materi Baru

Empat belas minggu sebelumnya telah memberikan banyak konsep.

Tetapi tujuan akhir mata kuliah bukan membuat mahasiswa mampu:

menyebutkan konsep-konsep komunikasi.

Tujuan akhirnya adalah:

menjadi communicator yang lebih baik.

Maka Minggu 15 bukan terutama:

lecture baru.

Minggu 15 adalah:

integration, performance, reflection, and demonstration of competence.

Pertanyaan final:

“When I face another person in a real situation, can I communicate well enough to create understanding, responsible action, and a healthier relationship?”

18.2 2. Perjalanan Satu Semester

Kita mulai dari pertanyaan yang sangat sederhana:

Who communicates with whom?

Kemudian framework berkembang.

18.2.1 Minggu 1 — Person

Who is the person?

18.2.2 Minggu 2 — Intra-Self

What am I telling myself?

18.2.3 Minggu 3 — Family & Friends

How do relationships grow and repair?

18.2.4 Minggu 4 — Language of Character

How should I speak to this person in this character?

18.2.5 Minggu 5 — Working Colleagues

What problem or value matters to the other person?

18.2.6 Minggu 6 — Target & Attention

Who is relevant and what deserves attention?

18.2.7 Minggu 7 — Interest

What possibility may help?

18.2.8 Minggu 8 — Midterm Performance

Can I actually communicate, listen, and adapt?

18.2.9 Minggu 9 — Desire

What future state does the person actually want?

18.2.10 Minggu 10 — Action

Can we turn desire into agreement and commitment?

18.2.11 Minggu 11 — Multi-Person Negotiation

Can several persons find an acceptable arrangement?

18.2.12 Minggu 12 — Community

Can diverse persons live and act together?

18.2.13 Minggu 13 — The World

Can one person communicate responsibly at scale?

18.2.14 Minggu 14 — AI-Mediated Communication

Can AI strengthen communication without replacing human agency and relationship?

18.2.15 Minggu 15

Can I integrate all of these into one complete communication performance?

18.3 3. Learning Objectives Minggu 15

Setelah menyelesaikan Minggu 15 mahasiswa mampu:

  1. mengintegrasikan seluruh person-centered communication framework;
  2. mengenali Person, Personality, Narrative Character, Relationship, dan Context;
  3. menetapkan communication objective yang legitimate;
  4. memilih repertoire dan language yang sesuai;
  5. menggunakan listening dan questioning sebelum membuat assumption;
  6. mengenali current communication state;
  7. menggunakan TAIDA secara adaptif, bukan sebagai script;
  8. menghasilkan shared understanding;
  9. membangun acceptable agreement dan coordinated action bila diperlukan;
  10. menjaga relationship ketika terjadi disagreement;
  11. menggunakan AI pada level yang appropriate;
  12. menunjukkan human judgment terhadap AI output;
  13. menilai success berdasarkan action dan relationship, bukan hanya persuasion;
  14. merefleksikan perkembangan communication character selama semester.

18.4 4. The Complete Framework

Seluruh semester dapat dirangkum:

PERSON ↓ PERSONALITY ↓ NARRATIVE CHARACTER ↓ CONTEXT & RELATIONSHIP ↓ OBJECTIVE ↓ REPERTOIRE ↓ LANGUAGE ↓ COMMUNICATION ↓ RESPONSE ↓ ADAPTATION ↓ SHARED UNDERSTANDING ↓ AGREEMENT ↓ ACTION ↓ CONSEQUENCE ↓ RELATIONSHIP

Dengan AI:

AI ASSISTANCE

dapat masuk di berbagai titik.

Tetapi:

Person tetap berada di pusat.

18.5 5. Person Before Message

Traditional communication model sering dimulai dari:

Sender → Message → Receiver.

Mata kuliah ini membuat perubahan penting:

Person A as Character A ⇄ Communication ⇄ Person B as Character B

Jadi communication bukan hanya:

pesan apa yang dikirim?

Tetapi:

siapa yang hadir?

sebagai siapa?

dengan relationship apa?

untuk objective apa?

pada situasi apa?

18.6 6. Personality Bukan Destiny

Personality membantu kita memahami:

tendencies.

Misalnya seseorang cenderung:

  • quiet,
  • analytical,
  • spontaneous,
  • careful,
  • expressive.

Tetapi personality tidak menentukan:

apa yang harus dilakukan.

Narrative Character memberi:

intentional direction.

Maka:

Personality gives tendencies. Character gives situated responsibility.

18.7 7. Narrative Character adalah Kunci

Person yang sama dapat berbicara sebagai:

  • friend,
  • student,
  • engineer,
  • manager,
  • customer,
  • citizen,
  • mentor,
  • leader.

Character mengubah:

objective,

language,

responsibility,

authority.

Pertanyaan communicator:

“Who am I being in this situation?”

dan:

“Who is the other person being?”

18.8 8. Relationship Mengubah Meaning

Kalimat yang sama memiliki meaning berbeda tergantung relationship.

“Kita perlu bicara.”

Dari:

  • supervisor,
  • spouse,
  • friend,
  • customer,

meaning-nya berbeda.

Communication competence membutuhkan:

relationship awareness.

18.9 9. Objective Before Language

Sebelum berbicara:

apa sebenarnya tujuan saya?

Buruk:

“Saya ingin menang.”

Lebih useful:

“Saya ingin memahami blocker dan membuat revised commitment.”

atau:

“Saya ingin memperbaiki relationship.”

atau:

“Saya ingin community dapat mencoba pilot yang fair.”

Objective mempengaruhi:

repertoire dan language.

18.10 10. The Complete Student Shorthand

Mahasiswa tidak perlu menghafal 130 slide.

Gunakan urutan sederhana:

KENALI ORANGNYA ↓ KENALI PERANNYA ↓ TAHU TUJUANMU ↓ DENGARKAN ↓ PILIH CARA DAN BAHASANYA ↓ KOMUNIKASIKAN ↓ LIHAT RESPONS ↓ SESUAIKAN ↓ SEPAKATI ↓ LAKUKAN ↓ LIHAT DAMPAK PADA RELASI

18.11 11. Communication Is a Closed Loop

Poor communication:

Think → Speak → Done

Learned communication:

Observe ↓ Interpret carefully ↓ Communicate ↓ Listen ↓ Observe response ↓ Update understanding ↓ Adapt

Communication bukan:

delivery.

Communication adalah:

interaction.

18.12 12. Listening is Not Waiting to Talk

Listening berarti mencoba memahami:

  • words,
  • meaning,
  • person,
  • objective,
  • concern.

Three levels:

WORDS → MEANING → PERSON

Excellent communicator tidak hanya mendengar:

apa yang dikatakan.

Ia mencoba memahami:

mengapa itu penting bagi person tersebut.

18.13 13. Fact, Story, and Assumption

Dari Minggu 2:

EVENT ≠ INTERPRETATION

Dari Minggu 12:

FACT ≠ INTERPRETATION ≠ VALUE ≠ POSITION

Ini adalah salah satu discipline terpenting dalam seluruh course.

Contoh:

Fact:

“Task dua hari terlambat.”

Story:

“Dia tidak peduli.”

Attack:

“Kamu memang malas.”

Learned communicator berhenti pada:

fact dulu,

lalu:

bertanya.

18.14 14. Repertoire

Communication bukan hanya speaking.

Repertoire mencakup:

  • listening,
  • questioning,
  • writing,
  • paraphrasing,
  • storytelling,
  • drawing,
  • demonstration,
  • data,
  • analogy,
  • silence,
  • negotiation,
  • prototype,
  • media,
  • AI.

Pertanyaan:

What method helps this person understand?

18.15 15. Language of Character

Satu technical reality dapat diterjemahkan.

Engineer:

“Database synchronization gagal.”

Manager:

“Dua sistem menghasilkan data yang tidak sama.”

Customer:

“Informasi yang Anda lihat bisa berbeda antara dua layar.”

Core meaning:

sama.

Language:

adapted.

18.16 16. Authenticity Tidak Berarti Berbicara Sama kepada Semua Orang

Authenticity bukan:

“Saya selalu bicara dengan cara yang sama.”

Kita berbicara berbeda kepada:

  • anak kecil,
  • professor,
  • customer,
  • friend.

Itu bukan fake.

Itu:

relational competence.

Prinsip:

Adapt expression without betraying meaning or values.

18.17 17. TAIDA — Complete Baseball Model

Kita mengingat:

TARGET → ATTENTION → INTEREST → DESIRE → ACTION

TAIDA bukan:

manipulation script.

TAIDA adalah:

state-awareness model.

18.18 18. TARGET

Pertanyaan:

Who is relevant?

Dalam person-centered TAIDA, target bukan:

demographic object.

Target adalah:

a person in a relevant role and situation.

18.19 19. ATTENTION

State:

“This matters to me.”

Pitch:

“Saya melihat ada problem/opportunity yang mungkin relevant bagi Anda.”

Attention dibangun melalui:

relevance + credible observation.

18.20 20. INTEREST

State:

“This may help.”

Pitch:

“Ada kemungkinan solution yang relevant.”

Fokus:

problem–solution fit.

18.21 21. DESIRE

State:

“I want that outcome.”

Pitch:

“Begini cara kerjanya dan seperti ini future state yang mungkin diperoleh.”

Fokus:

value + trust + risk.

18.22 22. ACTION

State:

“We agree to do this.”

Fokus:

  • feasibility,
  • authority,
  • negotiation,
  • commitment,
  • agreement.

18.23 23. TAIDA Bisa Lompat dan Mundur

Real communication tidak harus:

linear.

Person dapat sudah di Interest sebelum conversation.

Atau setelah melihat risk:

kembali ke Attention.

Good communicator bertanya:

Where is the person now?

bukan:

“Slide saya sekarang nomor berapa?”

18.24 24. Response Is Measurement

Jika target mengatakan:

“Saya tidak melihat itu sebagai masalah.”

Jangan terus pitching solution.

Measurement mengatakan:

Attention belum tercapai.

Jika target berkata:

“Bagaimana cara kerjanya?”

Itu signal:

Interest.

Communication menjadi:

feedback-controlled.

18.25 25. But Persons Are Not Machines

Walaupun kita memakai istilah:

state, feedback, control loop,

person bukan:

object to control.

Person mempunyai:

  • autonomy,
  • values,
  • judgment,
  • dignity,
  • freedom.

Karena itu:

communication state model helps us adapt communication, not control persons.

18.26 26. The Agreement Model

Ketika communication bergerak menuju action:

[ Agreement = WHO + WHAT + WHEN + EVIDENCE]

Tambahkan bila perlu:

CONDITIONS.

Contoh:

“Saya mengirim dataset Jumat pukul 15.00, Anda menyiapkan prototype Selasa, dan selesai berarti prototype dapat menjalankan 10 sample transactions.”

Itu jauh lebih kuat daripada:

“Nanti kita kerjakan.”

18.27 27. Agreement Tidak Selalu Berarti Consensus

Dua persons dapat berbeda pendapat tetapi setuju:

test dua alternatives.

Community dapat berbeda mengenai permanent policy tetapi sepakat:

pilot empat minggu.

Maka:

Agreement can coordinate disagreement.

18.28 28. Multi-Person Agreement

Dengan banyak stakeholders:

Person A, B, C, D

masing-masing mempunyai:

  • objective,
  • interest,
  • constraints,
  • authority.

Kita mencari:

acceptable configuration.

Secara konseptual:

[ A^* _{i=1}^{n} A_i]

Tidak harus:

perfect.

Harus:

sufficiently acceptable.

18.29 29. Position vs Interest

Position:

“Harus Jumat.”

Interest:

“Kami butuh demo Sabtu.”

Mengetahui interest membuka:

options.

Misalnya:

demoable core Jumat,

full version minggu depan.

Ini adalah communication sebagai:

value creation.

18.30 30. Community Communication

Dalam community:

complete agreement semakin sulit.

Maka targetnya dapat menjadi:

sufficient legitimate agreement for collective action.

Tetapi selalu tanyakan:

siapa mendapat benefit?

siapa menanggung burden?

siapa belum terdengar?

18.31 31. From Person vs Person to Persons vs Problem

Ini salah satu transformation penting course.

Buruk:

“Cashier menolak innovation.”

Lebih useful:

“Cashier khawatir workflow menjadi dua kali lebih rumit.”

Sekarang group dapat mengerjakan:

design problem.

18.32 32. Communicating with the World

Public communication memperluas scale.

ONE PERSON → MANY PERSONS

Tetapi “many” tetap:

persons.

Jangan hanya melihat:

  • followers,
  • impressions,
  • clicks.

Tanyakan:

apa yang mereka pahami?

apa yang berubah?

18.33 33. Reach ≠ Impact

Public outcome ladder:

REACH → ATTENTION → UNDERSTANDING → ENGAGEMENT → ADOPTION → ACTION → IMPACT

100.000 views:

bukan otomatis impact.

Communication competence berarti:

menghubungkan objective ke meaningful evidence.

18.34 34. Reputation

Reputation dapat dipahami sebagai:

relationship memory at scale.

Dibentuk dari:

communication + action + consequence over time.

Bukan sekadar:

branding.

18.35 35. AI Layer

Minggu 14 menambahkan:

AI assistance

pada seluruh system.

AI dapat menjadi:

  1. Mirror
  2. Coach
  3. Translator
  4. Copilot
  5. Mediator
  6. Delegate

18.36 36. AI Mirror

“Apa assumption dalam message saya?”

Meningkatkan:

self-awareness.

18.37 37. AI Coach

“Berperanlah sebagai customer yang skeptis.”

Meningkatkan:

preparation.

18.38 38. AI Translator

technical → business → public.

Meningkatkan:

accessibility.

18.39 39. AI Copilot

summarize meeting, retrieve information, identify unresolved points.

Meningkatkan:

coordination.

18.40 40. AI Mediator

membantu menerjemahkan conflict ke shared problem.

Meningkatkan:

mutual understanding.

18.41 41. AI Delegate

melakukan limited communication dalam authority tertentu.

Contoh:

scheduling.

Semakin consequential:

semakin diperlukan human control.

18.42 42. AI Does Not Own the Relationship

Core architecture:

[ (Person_A + AI_A) Communication (Person_B + AI_B)]

Tetapi:

[ Relationship = Person_A Person_B]

AI dapat membantu:

words.

Persons menanggung:

meaning, commitment, consequences.

18.43 43. The Messenger Can Be Part of the Message

“Terima kasih.”

“Maaf.”

“Saya bangga padamu.”

“Saya mencintaimu.”

Meaning-nya tidak hanya berada dalam:

words.

Meaning juga berada pada:

who said it.

18.70 70. Rubric 1 — Person & Character Recognition

Nilai tinggi:

  • recognizes person beyond category;
  • understands role;
  • notices relationship;
  • avoids stereotyping;
  • asks relevant questions.

Nilai rendah:

generic communication regardless of person.

18.71 71. Rubric 2 — Objective & State Recognition

Nilai tinggi:

  • objective clear;
  • identifies problem/state;
  • knows whether target is at Attention, Interest, Desire, etc.;
  • does not pitch wrong base.

Nilai rendah:

communicates without knowing what it is trying to achieve.

18.72 72. Rubric 3 — Repertoire, Language & AI Use

Nilai tinggi:

  • chooses appropriate language;
  • uses question/data/demo/analogy as needed;
  • uses AI appropriately;
  • preserves core meaning.

Nilai rendah:

  • jargon,
  • feature dumping,
  • AI output copied blindly.

18.73 73. Rubric 4 — Response, Listening & Adaptation

Ini mungkin dimensi paling penting.

Nilai tinggi:

target gives unexpected information → student adapts.

Nilai rendah:

target responds → student ignores → continues script.

18.74 74. Rubric 5 — Agreement, Action & Relationship

Nilai tinggi:

  • shared understanding clearer;
  • next action realistic;
  • agreement explicit where appropriate;
  • autonomy preserved;
  • relationship strengthened or responsibly maintained.

18.130 130. Final Ethical Principle

Knowledge of a person creates responsibility toward that person.

Semakin banyak data dan AI membantu kita memahami someone:

semakin besar tanggung jawab untuk tidak mengeksploitasi.

18.131 131. Final Communication Principle

Do not ask first: “How can I make this person agree with me?”

Ask:

“Who is this person?”

“What matters to them?”

“What do I genuinely know?”

“What are we trying to do?”

“What can we responsibly agree to?”

“What will this do to our relationship?”

18.133 133. Ten Final Take-Home Messages

Jika hanya mengingat sepuluh hal dari seluruh course:

  1. Communication begins with persons, not messages.
  2. Personality gives tendencies; narrative character gives intentional direction.
  3. The same person requires different language in different characters and relationships.
  4. Listen before explaining; understand the problem before proposing the solution.
  5. TAIDA helps recognize communication states: Target → Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.
  6. Agreement does not require identical thinking; it requires sufficient shared understanding for coordinated action.
  7. Good communication must consider both action outcome and relationship outcome.
  8. Communities require sufficient agreement, fair participation, and attention to who bears the burden.
  9. AI should augment human communication, not replace human agency, responsibility, or relationship.
  10. The mature communicator asks not only “Did I get what I wanted?” but also “Did we understand one another better, act responsibly, and leave the relationship healthier?”

18.134 134. Closing Statement for the Course

Pada hari pertama kita dapat mengira communication adalah:

how to speak well.

Setelah 15 minggu kita memahami sesuatu yang lebih besar.

Communication adalah kemampuan person untuk:

mengenali dirinya,

mengenali person lain,

memilih character yang appropriate,

menemukan language yang dapat dipahami bersama,

mendengar response,

mengelola difference,

membangun agreement,

melakukan coordinated action,

dan memelihara relationship.

Maka:

Good communication is not merely the art of making oneself understood.

Ia adalah:

the discipline of creating shared understanding among persons sufficiently well that they can live, work, decide, and act together without losing their dignity and relationship.

Dan di era AI:

AI dapat membuat kita berbicara lebih cepat, menulis lebih indah, menerjemahkan lebih banyak, dan menganalisis lebih dalam. Tetapi kita tetap harus belajar menjadi manusia yang tahu kepada siapa kita berbicara, mengapa kita berbicara, kapan harus mendengar, apa yang layak dijanjikan, dan relationship apa yang harus kita jaga sesudah kata-kata selesai.

Itulah The Complete Person-Centered Communicator.
